# OWHS decision log

*The standard's own public decision log starts here; entries D1 onward ship with the repository at launch. Format: decision, options considered, resolution, reasons, who, date.*

| # | Decision | Resolution | Reasons | Who | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Name and naming stack | **OWHS (Open Workplace Health Standard)** as the standard's formal name; **Open Work Health** as the project umbrella, domain (openworkhealth.org) and GitHub org; `owhs:` namespace unchanged. *Umbrella and domain superseded by D7 same day* | The technical artefacts already use the owhs namespace; acronym for precision, plain English for the front door; owhs.org unavailable made the split natural | Founder | 7 Jul 2026 |
| D2 | The `individual-employer` visibility class (schema's most contestable call) | **Keep** | Without it, OH opinions and Equality Act adjustments become aggregate statistics rather than usable records; exposure bounded: fields exhaustively enumerated, legal basis required, no clinical content, schema-enforced. The privacy-hardliner alternative (aggregate-only everywhere) was considered and rejected as gutting the OH entity | Founder | 7 Jul 2026 |
| D3 | OHEpisode placement | **Core**, not a profile | OH is what SMEs buy for exactly the hard cases; the referral→opinion envelope is vendor-universal; the clinical-content ban is schema-enforced. Statutory surveillance stays in the `owhs-ohsurveillance` profile | Founder | 7 Jul 2026 |
| D4 | Sustained-RTW checkpoint encoding | **Open integer (1 to 104 weeks) with a recommended set {4, 13, 26}**, replacing the hard enum | The windows are convention, not an anchored UK definition; enum-locking a guess the WHIU may overwrite invited a breaking change. Schema edited and re-verified against valid/invalid examples same day | Founder | 7 Jul 2026 |
| D5 | Safeguarding-category list ownership | **Provisional v0.1 list seeded from the IWE safeguarding taxonomy (categories only, no data), owned by the steward; ratification designated one of the first formal acts of the co-steward governance body** | The line between leaking sensitive signal and suppressing legitimate risk data is a policy call, not an implementer one; launching with a named provisional list beats launching with a hole. New code list `safeguarding-category@0.1.0` added to the registry | Founder | 7 Jul 2026 |
| D6 | Pre-launch web presence | **Quietly live**: the project domain fronting the one-pager (domain per D7), noindex, mailto instead of repo links until launch | Gives inner-circle conversations something real; invisible to search; honest about pre-release status in the copy | Founder | 7 Jul 2026 |
| D7 | Naming revision: retire the "Open Work Health" umbrella | **The standard is simply the Open Workplace Health Standard (OWHS)**; home domain **openworkplacehealth.org** (+ .com/.uk/.co.uk/.org.uk defensive set, owhs.org.uk acronym redirect, owhstandard.org), GitHub org `openworkplacehealth`. No separate project brand | Due diligence on the earlier choice found openworkhealth.com held by a US healthcare staffing firm and, more materially, "Openwork" is a major UK FCA-regulated financial-advice brand (The Openwork Partnership) adjacent to the steward's own industry; "Open Work Health" as a lead token invited a collision the descriptive full name avoids entirely. Full-name domain and its .com were both available, giving a cleaner position than the original basket | Founder | 7 Jul 2026 |
