Who uses OWHS

No organisation is listed yet. This is the public register of organisations that say they use, or have mapped data to, the Open Workplace Health Standard.

What being listed means

  • It is a declaration by the organisation that it uses, or has mapped to, a named part of the standard, the registry or the question bank.
  • It is not certification. A listing does not say that an organisation conforms at any level. An entry can link a public result, such as a validation report; the register shows the link and does not treat it as a test of the claim.
  • It is not an endorsement by the project of the organisation, or by the organisation of the project.
  • Interests are declared in public. Contributors to OWHS are asked to declare any financial or professional interest, and those declarations are public (see governance). Every entry carries one, including an organisation that sells a product implementing OWHS.

Independent use is also one of the evidence points the project's governance page names for moving to multi-stakeholder governance, which is one reason to keep an honest register: an empty one is more useful than an inflated one.

How to be added

  • Open the Add my organisation form on GitHub. It asks for the name, a website, what you use and a declaration of interest. The issue is public.
  • Or email hello@openworkplacehealth.org if you would rather not raise it in public.

Do not send real records or personal data. If you tried a mapping and it failed, a mapping report is worth as much as a listing.
