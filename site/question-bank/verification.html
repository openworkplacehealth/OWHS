Every item's wording is either diffed against its primary source or not reproduced at all. Nothing is paraphrased.

The four ONS personal well-being items were matched against the ONS source in v0.1.0; the Eurofound, Civil Service People Survey and Annual Population Survey items were matched in v0.2.0. Held items carry no wording, so there is nothing to diff: the last column says why each one is held.
