Evidence sweep · October 2026

cycle 2026-10 · no verified sweep · filed 19 November 2026 · registry v0.9.0, unchanged by this page

No verified evidence sweep exists for October 2026. The planned harvest for cycle 2026-10 did not complete, so there is no candidates file for the month and nothing from the harvest was screened.

What happened

The harvest started on 1 October 2026 (run 36862246228). October is a quarterly month, so besides the monthly publication window the names and citation channels also ran with no date filter, over the full publication history. The run was cancelled after six hours, the job time limit, and wrote no artefact. On 3 October the tripwire could not verify the cycle and opened issue #63, which is still open.

Why the month cannot be recovered

Under the cycle's own rules a planned cycle is satisfied only by a complete, full-inventory run of that cycle, correlated with its own workflow issue. A later run with an explicit window is a manual cycle and never satisfies a planned one. Cycle 2026-10 will therefore stay unverified, and this page does not claim otherwise.

No watermark has ever advanced, so each planned run searches publication dates from the first day of the previous month. October's window would have covered works published in September 2026. The November run's publication window began on 1 October, so works published in September were searched in November only through Europe PMC's first-index catch-up (works first indexed from 3 August to 1 November 2026), not through the citation channels. The full-history pass that October's quarterly run should have made has not been made by any later run.

Studies from the period picked up later

A separate monthly assisted session, working from web search only and not from any harvester candidates file, proposed two studies in a draft dated 1 October and three more in a draft dated 1 November. On 19 November each of the five was checked against Crossref (title, authors, year, journal and any update notice), its DOI was resolved, and its abstract was read. All five were added through the public screening gate on 19 November and are reported on the November sweep page. One of them was published inside October's window (the Slovak UWES-9 study, 28 September 2026); the other four were published earlier, between November 2025 and April 2026.

  • PHQ-9: Flores-Kanter and colleagues, BMC Psychiatry, 2026 (10.1186/s12888-026-07820-9). First proposed in the October draft.
  • UWES-9: Heveri and colleagues, Scientific Reports, 2025 (10.1038/s41598-025-26907-z). First proposed in the October draft.
  • UWES-9: Nosáľová and Dudová, Studia Psychologica, 2026 (10.31577/sp.2026.03.948). Proposed in the November draft.

None of these was found by the harvester. They show what the missing October run might have caught; they are not a substitute for it, and a web search is not a systematic search.

Stated limits

Nothing on this page is a search result for October. Silence on an instrument for this month means it was not searched, not that nothing was published. No grade moved.
