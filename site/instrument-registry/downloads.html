Downloads

The registry is published as a canonical JSON dataset, a grade-matrix CSV, and a printable PDF.

  • instrument-evidence-base-v0.8.0.json: the canonical dataset (31 records at schema v0.7: full findings, grades with evidence state, rubric version and review flags, provenance, licence classes with archived steward pages, citations, corrections, errata, right of reply).
  • instrument-evidence-matrix-full.csv: the 27-instrument grade matrix, generated from the dataset at each build, with the criterion split and each cell's indirectness flag or absence type.
  • RUBRIC-v1.5.md: the grading rubric every cell cites.
  • ADMISSION-v1.1.md: the admission rule, how an instrument gets a record at all.
  • instrument-registry.pdf: a printable export of the matrix and record summaries.

Relations between records (item-of, short-form-of, screens-for, corresponds-with, embeds) are in the dataset as relations on each record, forward only, each with the study it rests on; the single-item measures page renders them.

How to cite

Open Workplace Health Standard, Instrument Registry, dataset v0.8.0 (2026-09-03). Available at openworkplacehealth.org. Grades are editorial assessments of published evidence for a working-adult audience; see the methodology page for the grading conventions.

Licensing

Registry text is licensed CC BY 4.0; the dataset structure is licensed Apache 2.0. Instrument item text is never reproduced in this registry; each instrument's own licence status is recorded on its page, verified against the steward's current terms where possible.

OWHS Instrument Registry, dataset version 0.8.0 (schema 0.7), generated 2026-09-03; grades last confirmed 2026-09-03 under rubric v1.5. A resource maintained under the Open Workplace Health Standard, distinct from the normative specification. Grades are editorial assessments of published evidence for a working-adult audience in any country; they are not part of the standard and change through the registry's public corrections process, not by RFC. Grades are single-rater and frozen from first publication until independent raters join (see who graded this).
