Rubric v1.6: the norm rule is read as written, so a High grade on populations, languages and norms rests on a study that states a norm, cut-off, prevalence or reference value and not on a word that matches the test; section 10 says that precondition evidence sits only on a High cell or a High previous block. The WHO-5 populations cell notes that its one norm-bearing entry is a cut-off validated in diabetes patients and holds on its working-adult entries. One statistic gains a population clause with its value unchanged. All 420 DOIs resolved on the conformance date. Schema 0.7 unchanged. Every changed cell keeps its 1.5 values in previous. Logged as correction C-0009.
v0.8.03 Sep 2026Reconciliation of the fourth independent check, of v0.7.0, before first publication; the check's verdict was fit to publish and its ten findings were non-blocking, every one of them taken. Rubric v1.5: a High grade on populations, languages and norms rests on at least one counted study carrying a norm, cut-off, prevalence or explicitly reported reference value, and the build tests it (every current cell holds); criterion validity is two lists, so a correlation or regression coefficient counts only against organisational outcomes; the rubric's statistic lists and the machine check are reconciled in both directions, a comma bounds a clause, and one entry's statistic is reworded with its value unchanged; section 10 gains a row for every check the build already ran. One grade moved, European Working Conditions Survey internal consistency Moderate to Low (no reliability coefficient of the record's own; the embedded WHO-5's alpha belongs to the WHO-5 record). The K10 criterion-validity judgement is rewritten, grade unchanged. All 420 DOIs resolved on the conformance date. Schema 0.7 unchanged. Correction C-0008. Fifth pass, scoped to the diff, pending before the noindex lift.
v0.7.02 Sep 2026Reconciliation of the third independent check, of v0.6.0, before first publication; the check found no grade demonstrably wrong. Rubric v1.4: the High precondition names the statistics of each property and the build tests every cited study against its own property's list (nine studies leave the tables on four cells; every one of those cells keeps two or more and stays High); high_basis is renamed precondition_evidence, the studies that meet the precondition, and a cell that drops from High keeps that evidence in its history; internal consistency on item sets and derived indices is stated; a flag descriptor may name a population by the survey, trial or organisation that sampled it; a direct reason lists a working-adult sample first; the rule that borrowed evidence never grades High is now checked; every rule names its check and the unchecked ones are listed. One High grade drops to Moderate (k10 criterion validity against a reference standard, on a credible published disagreement, the rater's judgement written on the cell), two internal-consistency cells become Not-applicable (wpai, fcs-maps), two population tags and two evidence forms are corrected. All 420 DOIs resolved. Schema 0.7: eight-field previous blocks. Logged as correction C-0007 with the rubric 1.3 values kept on each changed cell. No fresh literature search: as-of dates unchanged.
v0.6.02 Sep 2026Reconciliation of the second independent check, of v0.5.0, before first publication. Rubric v1.3: the evidence-form rule recodes a cell to the form its findings attribute instead of capping its grade, so a cell is mixed only where the findings name two forms claim by claim; a High grade needs two cited studies with a sample size and a statistic of the property graded, and on a population-sensitive property at least one of them in a working-adult or general-population sample, with every cited study tagged by population; criterion validity against a reference standard joins the population-sensitive properties (diagnostic accuracy depends on the sample); every flag descriptor names a population. One Moderate returns to High (k10 internal consistency, capped in v0.5.0 on a coding error), five High cells drop to Moderate, four single-item invariance cells and one derived-index structure cell become Not-applicable, three flags move, 24 cells are recoded off mixed, 29 cited studies leave the High-basis tables for want of a statistic of the property. Schema 0.6: population on High-basis entries, two absence types, seven-field previous blocks, structured abstract contradictions. One gate module now runs in both the migration and the site build. All logged as correction C-0006 with the rubric 1.2 values kept on each changed cell. No fresh literature search: as-of dates unchanged.
v0.5.02 Sep 2026Reconciliation of the independent check of v0.4.0, before first publication. Rubric v1.2: the indirectness flag has three values (direct, general, indirect), each cell states the studies its flag rests on, a grade never moves because a flag moved, and a High grade needs at least two cited studies with a sample size and a statistic, listed on the cell. The six C-0004 grade moves are reversed and six further High cells drop to Moderate (three for want of the numbers, two for indirect evidence on a population-sensitive property, one for a mixed cell that names no second form); 49 flags change; six criterion cells that had been graded on a reference standard the construct does not have become Absent (category error); four not-applicable cells become Absent and one Absent cell becomes not-applicable. Every Absent or Not-applicable cell names its absence type and carries no flag. Schema 0.5: indirectness_basis, absence_type, high_basis, a corresponds-with relation for single items whose only published link to an instrument is a correlation, and a generated integrity block. The site build now fails on any cell that breaks these rules. All logged as correction C-0005 with the rubric 1.1 values kept on each changed cell. No fresh literature search: as-of dates unchanged.
v0.4.02 Sep 2026Purpose reset before first publication. The registry's purpose is stated: the open synthesis of the published evidence on instruments used to measure workplace health and wellbeing, scope the whole field by construct, the first 27 instruments stage one. Rubric v1.1: indirectness is defined by population type, never by country or language; every cell re-read, 92 flags changed and 6 grades moved (all upward, all occupational instruments), logged as correction C-0004 with the rubric 1.0 values kept on each cell. Schema 0.4: every indirectness flag carries its reason; relations on every record link single items and short forms to the multi-item instruments they were validated against, each citing its study; new single-item measures page. Freeze dated from first publication. Search venues and strings published on the methods page. No fresh literature search: as-of dates unchanged.
v0.3.02 Sep 2026Schema 0.3. Every graded cell now carries an evidence state (assessed, assessed_absent, not_assessed, not_applicable), the rubric version it was graded under, the date its literature was last searched, the date its grade was last confirmed by a rater, and a review-due flag. Grading rubric v1.0 published and linked from every cell. Licence figures replaced by a licence class for employer or vendor use, with archived copies of steward pages linked on each record. Single-rater disclosure recorded in the dataset: grades and statuses frozen from 1 Sep 2026 until two named non-employee raters join. Errata list and right-of-reply policy added. Steward-product disclosure field added, not yet populated. No grades moved. Migration script published beside the dataset.
